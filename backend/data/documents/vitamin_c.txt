Topic: Vitamin C

Vitamin C is an essential dietary component and is required for several important biological functions.

Vitamin C is naturally present in fruits and vegetables and is also available as a dietary supplement.

Vitamin C plays roles in collagen production, antioxidant activity, immune function, and iron absorption.

Evidence for vitamin C varies across different health conditions, and vitamin C supplements should not be considered a universal treatment or cure for all illnesses.

Source organization: National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements
