Title: Physical Activity Overview

Source: World Health Organization

Source URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity

Topic: Exercise

Regular physical activity provides significant physical and mental health benefits.

In adults, physical activity contributes to the prevention and management of noncommunicable diseases such as cardiovascular diseases, cancer, and diabetes.

Physical activity can improve cardiovascular fitness and overall well-being.

Exercise is beneficial for health, but it should not be considered a guaranteed cure for every disease.
