Topic: Diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic disease that occurs when the pancreas does not produce enough insulin or when the body cannot effectively use the insulin it produces.

Type 2 diabetes is the most common form of diabetes and is influenced by factors including genetics, overweight and obesity, physical inactivity, and dietary and other environmental factors.

Diabetes can be managed through lifestyle measures, medicines when appropriate, and regular monitoring of blood glucose levels.
