Title: Hydration Overview

Source: World Health Organization

Source URL: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015241

Topic: Hydration

Water is essential for normal body functions.

Sufficient water intake is necessary to replace water losses and maintain normal water balance in the body.

Dehydration occurs when the body does not receive enough water to replace its losses and can have adverse health effects.

Maintaining adequate hydration is important for health and well-being.
